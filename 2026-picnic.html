UPAD Summer Milan & Picnic 2026

A Day of Food, Fun & Friendship for the Entire Family
Sunday, August 23, 2026 · 12:00 PM – 4:00 PM · Marshbank Park, West Bloomfield
📍 West Bloomfield Township, MI 48324, USA

Join the UP Association of Detroit for our most anticipated summer gathering — a day of delicious food, family fun, and the joy of reconnecting with our community.

Whether you're a long-time UPAD member or joining us for the first time, come spend a relaxing afternoon by the beautiful lake at Marshbank Park. Catch up with old friends, welcome new families, let the kids enjoy the playground and sports courts, and create wonderful memories together.

Event Highlights

  • Beautiful lakeside setting at Marshbank Park
  • Live Food Stations
    Jalebi • Pakora • Corn • Biryani • Chole Bhature
  • Volleyball, games, playground & family activities
  • Bingo & Antakshari
  • Networking
    Meet fellow UPAD families through city introductions
  • Meet old friends, make new ones, and celebrate our shared heritage
